## The rule

**1. Redistributing it unchanged.** Passing along this repository, or an artifact we published,
exactly as we published it — call it RistOS. That is what it is.

A build you make yourself from an unmodified tag, with no patches of your own, is close enough that
we will not object either. The moment you patch anything, rule 2 applies.

**2. A modified build is not RistOS.** Change the code, the bundled apps, the defaults, the keys it
trusts, the backend it points at — anything — and the result must carry a name of your own.

The test, so you do not have to ask us:

> A name is fine if a reader could not take it for a name **we** chose. In practice: do not use
> "Rist" as your name, as the first word of it, or as its distinctive part, and do not use anything
> a reader would see or hear as "RistOS".

| Fine | Not fine |
|---|---|
| `Fenwick OS`, `Halyard`, any name of your own | `RistOS Pro`, `RistOS Community Edition` |
| "Fenwick OS, based on RistOS" | `Rist OS`, `R1stOS`, `RistOS-NG` |
| "a fork of RistOS" · "built from RistOS source" | `MyRist`, `RistPhone`, `rist-os.example.com` |
| "compatible with RistOS" | a logo of ours, recoloured |

Same rule for domains, repository names, app-store listings, social handles and product packaging:
it is the identifying name that matters, not where it appears.

**One exception.** The Android application ID is `watch.rist.assistant`. That is a technical
identifier, not a name anyone reads, and changing it forces a device wipe.
**We do not ask forks to change it.**

**3. Saying true things about us is always allowed.** Naming RistOS to describe what your thing is,
what it came from, or what it works with, needs no permission from anybody. Neither does criticism,
comparison, or a review. Keep it accurate and do not imply we endorsed, tested or supplied it.

**4. Logos and boot artwork.** Ship them only with an unmodified build under rule 1. Take them out
of anything else. Do not alter them and keep calling them ours.
